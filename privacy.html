Privacy Policy

Effective as of November 4, 2026

This privacy policy applies to the Nestory app for mobile devices, operated by Berk Yavuz (the "Service Provider"). It describes what happens to your data when you use Nestory.

The short version

Nestory has no server and no user accounts. Your memories — photos, notes, dates, and locations — are stored only on your own device and, if you're on iOS, backed up to your own iCloud account. The Service Provider never sees, receives, or stores your content anywhere.

What Nestory stores, and where

  • Your memories (place name, coordinates, note text, date, and a reference to the photo(s) you attached) are stored in a local database on your device.
  • On iOS, this same information is automatically backed up to your own iCloud account (in your app's private iCloud container), so it can be restored if you reinstall the app or set up a new device signed into the same iCloud account. This happens automatically once you're signed into iCloud on your device — the Service Provider has no access to this backup.
  • On Android, if you connect your Google account, the same information is backed up to your own Google Drive (in a private app folder only Nestory can see). Nestory uses Google Sign-In only to get permission to write that backup; the Service Provider has no access to it. You can disconnect at any time in your Google account settings.
  • Photos are never copied or uploaded anywhere by Nestory. When you add a photo to a memory, Nestory stores a reference to that photo in your device's own photo library and displays it directly from there. If you later delete that photo from your photo library, or turn off photo access for Nestory, the photo will no longer display in that memory (the note, date, and location are unaffected).
  • Location data you choose to attach to a memory (either picked manually on the map or from your device's current location) is stored the same way — locally, and backed up to your own iCloud on iOS. Nestory does not track your location in the background or at any time you are not actively creating or viewing a memory.

Nothing is sent to the Service Provider

Nestory does not operate a server. There is no account to create, no email or password to provide, and no database that the Service Provider or Nestory has access to. The Service Provider cannot see, read, or recover your memories — only you, through your own device and your own iCloud account, can.

Purchases (Nestory Pro)

Nestory Pro is an optional yearly subscription sold through the App Store or Google Play. Payment is handled entirely by Apple or Google; the Service Provider never sees your name, email, or payment details.

To check whether your subscription is active, Nestory uses RevenueCat (RevenueCat, Inc.), a subscription service. When the app starts or you make a purchase, RevenueCat receives a random, anonymous app user ID generated on your device, your purchase receipt from Apple or Google (product, purchase date, renewal status), and basic technical details such as app version, device model, OS version, country/currency and language. RevenueCat does not receive your memories, photos, notes, or locations. See RevenueCat's privacy policy.

No analytics, no tracking, no advertising

Apart from the purchase check described above, Nestory does not use any third-party analytics, crash reporting, advertising, or tracking services. No advertising identifiers are collected, and nothing is used to track you across apps or websites.

Permissions Nestory asks for

  • Photo Library access: so you can pick photos from your gallery to attach to a memory, and so Nestory can display them. Nestory only reads photos you explicitly select — it does not scan or access your library otherwise.
  • Location access: so you can pin a memory to your current location with one tap, or search for a place on the map. Location is only read when you actively use these features.

You can grant or revoke either permission at any time in your device's Settings. Revoking Photo Library access will make photos in your existing memories stop displaying, as described above.

No social features, no sharing

Nestory has no accounts, no profiles, no followers, no likes, and no way to share a memory with anyone through the app. Your memories are private to you by design.

No cross-platform sync

iOS and Android use separate, unrelated cloud ecosystems (iCloud and Google Drive), and Nestory does not bridge them. A memory created on an iPhone will not currently appear on an Android device, or vice versa.

Children

Nestory is not directed at children and does not knowingly collect information from children, and does not collect any personal information beyond the anonymous purchase check described above.

Data deletion

Deleting a memory in the app removes it from your device and, on iOS, from your iCloud backup. Uninstalling the app removes all local data; your iCloud backup (if any) can be managed or removed via your device's iCloud storage settings, since it lives in your own account, not the Service Provider's.

Changes to this policy

If this policy changes, this page will be updated with a new effective date.
